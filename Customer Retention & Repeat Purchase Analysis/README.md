# Customer Retention & Repeat Purchase Analysis

SQL analysis of customer repeat-purchase behavior for **Willow & Co**, an online home-goods retailer.

## Business Question

> Are customers coming back to purchase again, and which acquisition channels have stronger repeat behavior?

## Key Findings

* **Overall repeat-customer rate:** 55.70%
* **Organic Search:** 75.00%
* **Email:** 70.83%
* **Direct:** 66.67%
* **Referral:** 52.38%
* **Paid Social:** 20.51%

Paid Social showed a significantly lower repeat rate than other acquisition channels, making it the primary area for further investigation.

## Data & Analysis

* 150 customers
* 301 orders
* SQL-based analysis
* Duplicate and inconsistent data handled
* Conditional aggregation and customer-level analysis
* Acquisition and retention metrics analyzed separately

## Key Takeaway

The overall retention rate alone hides major differences between acquisition channels. **Channel-level analysis revealed Paid Social as the main retention gap.**
